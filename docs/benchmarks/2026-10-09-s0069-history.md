# Sector 69 benchmark — history of 2026-10-09

Frozen list: 220 ExoFOP TOIs (CP/KP/PC, Planet SNR ≥ 10, 0.5 < P ≤ 13 d) with sector 69.
185 have an SPOC 2-min light curve. 135 are **detectable in one sector**: expected SNR ≥ 7.1 and ≥ 2 epochs with data.

The ExoFOP "Planet SNR" is multi-sector. In the first run, 55 of the 68 missed TOIs had expected SNR < 7 with sector 69 alone. That is why the main metric became recall on the detectable ones.

Machine: 16 CPUs, 15 processes, TLS 2.0 backend `fused-pl` with 1 thread per target.

## Causes found and fixed

| Cause | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| ±30 % limits on R and M cut off transits shorter than the central one | TOI 196.01, 347.01, 386.01, 2339.01 without signal; with wide limits SDE 8.6–16.8 | `_stellar_kwargs`: always wide limits |
| 1.0 d window leaves fast variability | TOI 862.01 (5092 ppm): std/p2p = 2.8; with 0.5 d SDE 18 | adaptive variant when std/p2p > 1.2 |
| Short window eats long transits | TOI 190.01 (5.7 h): 8248 ppm become 347 ppm with 0.3 d | search both variants and merge |
| TLS derives the minimum period (Roche) from the model R | R_TIC = 4.06 made the grid start at 4.15 d; TOI 196.01 has 1.16 d | model capped at R, M ≤ 1.5 |
| P/3 with 2 transits and data gaps | TOI 899.01, 924.01, 3262.01 found at ~4.2 d | `refine_alias`: accepts kP if epochs belonging only to P have data without a transit |
